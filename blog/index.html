Why These Guides Matter

Most teams still build presentations by hand: opening a spreadsheet, copying a row, pasting it into a slide, and repeating the process dozens of times. It is slow, error-prone, and boring. Automation changes that. With the right template and a clean spreadsheet, a hundred presentations can be produced in seconds instead of hours.

These tutorials focus on practical, repeatable workflows. You do not need to write code. You need a spreadsheet, a PowerPoint template, and a few minutes of setup.
