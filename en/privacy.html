Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Merge It website and the Merge It desktop application (together, "the Service") handle information. Merge It is developed and maintained by Man Singh Rana (TsGuideMan).

1. The Merge It desktop application

  • Merge It runs entirely offline on your computer. Your spreadsheets and PowerPoint templates are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server.
  • The application does not require an account, login, or personal information.
  • The application does not collect, transmit, or sell your file contents or usage data.
  • No internet connection is required to use the application.

2. Information collected on this website

  • Cookies: this website may use essential cookies (for example, to remember your cookie-notice preference).
  • Analytics: if a web analytics service (such as Google Analytics) is installed by the site owner, it may collect anonymized usage data such as pages visited, browser type, and approximate country. No personal identity is collected.
  • Log data: the hosting provider may automatically record information such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested for security and error handling.

3. Advertising and cookies

  • This website may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (for example Google AdSense).
  • Advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • We do not control the practices of third-party advertisers. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of those parties.

4. Third-party links

This website links to third-party services such as GitHub, Facebook, and YouTube. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please see the Contact page or open an issue on the project repository.
